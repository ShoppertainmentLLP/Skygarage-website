Mercedes-Benz servicing splits neatly into two categories, and knowing which one your car needs
is most of the value an independent specialist adds.

## The routine half

Service A and Service B alternate, and the car itself reports which is due and when. A workshop
reading that data services what the car asks for. One without it sells a package that is either
more than you needed or quietly less.

Beyond the schedule, the item most often missed on Mercedes here is transmission fluid. The 7G
and 9G gearboxes are widely described as sealed for life, and in stop-start Dubai traffic at 45°C
that claim ages badly. A fluid and filter service every 60,000 to 80,000 km is inexpensive
insurance on a very expensive component.

## The electronics half

Anything else on a modern Mercedes is likely to be electronic, and this is where a generic OBD
reader stops being useful. Comfort modules, air suspension control, driver assistance systems and
the central gateway all store their own faults, and reaching them takes manufacturer-level
software.

That is the reason requests for this make go to workshops that hold it rather than to any
all-makes garage.

## Where quotes differ

The same three things every time: the labour rate, whether parts are genuine or OEM, and what is
actually included. Ask about all three and the remaining difference is genuinely price.
