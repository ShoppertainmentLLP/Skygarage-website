Toyotas are the easiest cars in this market to have serviced well and the easiest to overpay for,
for the same reason: everybody can do it.

## Use the competition

Almost every workshop in the UAE services Toyotas competently, and parts are stocked everywhere.
That makes this the make where sending one request to several garages produces the largest
percentage saving — the same scheduled service can vary by several hundred dirhams between
workshops five minutes apart, with no difference in the work.

## Sand changes the maintenance

A Land Cruiser or Prado that regularly leaves tarmac is on a different schedule from one that
does the school run.

Air filters clog quickly enough that a book interval is meaningless. Sand works into suspension
components and brake hardware. The underbody deserves a look after any serious trip, and the
cooling system deserves attention because low-speed sand driving is hard work with very little
airflow.

None of this is expensive. It is just more frequent than the manual suggests.

## Longevity is real, and conditional

These engines pass 300,000 km routinely when fluids are changed on time. What tends to fail
first is everything around them: bushes, shocks, brake hardware, AC components and cooling.

Which is a good argument for spending on inspection rather than only on the service itself — the
mechanical parts will keep going if the wear items are caught.

## Hybrids

The engine half is ordinary work. The hybrid half is not, and needs a workshop with high-voltage
training and the ability to read the battery's state of health. Ask before booking; the garages
here state which are equipped.
