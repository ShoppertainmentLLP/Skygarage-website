Ferrari ownership in Dubai splits into two categories of work with very different economics.

## The annual half

Fluids, filters, brake inspection, a diagnostic scan and a proper look over the car. This is
routine work for a specialist, and it is where independents are dramatically cheaper than main
dealers for identical results.

In this climate the annual part matters more than the mileage part. Brake fluid absorbs moisture
and loses boiling point regardless of how far the car has travelled, and on a car with this much
performance a fluid failure is not an inconvenience.

## The clutch question

Uniquely useful on these cars: remaining clutch life is a measurable percentage that a specialist
can read in minutes.

That single figure is worth knowing before buying a used one, before a season of track days, and
before accepting anybody's opinion that the clutch is "getting tired". It converts a guess into a
number.

## Track use

At Dubai Autodrome, everything accelerates. Brake fluid, pads, oil and tyres become consumables
counted in sessions. A pre-event inspection covering fluids, brakes, geometry and fasteners is
routine practice rather than caution.

## Where an independent should stop

Engine-out work and anything requiring factory-only tooling belongs elsewhere, and the useful
signal is a workshop that says so unprompted. The specialists here will tell you which side of
that line your job falls on before quoting.
