Lamborghini in Dubai is now two quite different ownership propositions, and the servicing market
has caught up with that.

## The Urus is not exotic to maintain

It shares its platform and a great deal of its engineering with other large Volkswagen Group
SUVs. A specialist who understands that services one competently at a price closer to a big
German SUV than to a supercar, using parts that are widely available.

That is the single most useful thing an Urus owner in this market can know, because the default
assumption pushes people towards main-dealer pricing for work that does not require it.

## The mid-engined cars

Huracán and Aventador servicing is routine for a specialist: fluids, filters, brakes, an
inspection, a scan. What it requires is the right diagnostic access and — genuinely — the right
lift, because ground clearance on these cars is not something to improvise around.

## The lift system

Nose-lift is the most-exercised option on any low car in Dubai. Every ramp, every speed bump,
several times a day.

Unsurprisingly, it is also one of the most common repairs. The system has pumps, valves, sensors
and lines that fail individually, which makes diagnosis worth paying for rather than accepting a
quote for the assembly.

## Fluids on the calendar

Heat degrades brake fluid whether the car is driven or parked. On cars with this performance,
annual fluid changes are the cheapest insurance available, and any specialist will put them on
the calendar rather than the odometer.
