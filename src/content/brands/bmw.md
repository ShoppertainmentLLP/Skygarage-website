BMWs reward a workshop that reads the car and punish one that guesses. That is the whole
difference between a good independent BMW specialist in Dubai and a general all-makes garage that
will happily take the booking.

## What the car already knows

Every modern BMW tracks the condition of its own oil, brakes, filters and fluids and reports how
much life is left in each. A workshop with the right software reads that, services what is
actually due, and shows you the remaining figures afterwards.

A shop without it sells a package. Sometimes the package is more than the car needed, sometimes
it quietly skips something the car was asking for.

## Where BMW money goes in this climate

Cooling. Almost everything expensive on an F-series BMW in Dubai starts as a cooling problem —
the electric water pump, the thermostat housing, the expansion tank. Ambient heat that sits above
40°C for months puts these components under a load they were not designed around, and a slow
coolant loss becomes a warped head if it is ignored.

The second is the timing chain on early N20 four-cylinder engines, which is a known issue with a
known fix, and enormously cheaper to address before it fails than after.

The third is air suspension on the cars that have it. A compressor working overtime against a
tired air spring is a repair that grows if you leave it.

## Genuine, OEM, or neither

BMW does not manufacture most of its own parts. The same water pump, in the same factory, goes
into a BMW box and a supplier's box at very different prices. For some components — anything
electronic, anything the car has to recognise — the genuine part is the right answer. For plenty
of others it is not.

Being shown both prices and asked which you want is the mark of a workshop that intends to keep
you as a customer.

## Choosing between quotes

When three BMW quotes come back at different numbers, the difference is almost always one of
three things: the labour rate, whether the parts are genuine or OEM, and what has actually been
included. Compare those three and what is left is genuinely price.
