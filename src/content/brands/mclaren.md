McLaren servicing is a short list of jobs done precisely, by a small number of workshops.

## What the list actually contains

Fluids on an annual cycle. Brake and hydraulic suspension inspection. Battery condition. A full
diagnostic scan across systems that a generic tool cannot see. Tyres, which on these cars age out
long before they wear out unless the car is used hard.

That is most of it. These are not maintenance-heavy cars in the traditional sense; they are cars
where the few required items genuinely matter.

## The parked-car problem

Most McLarens in this market cover very low mileage, and low use creates its own failure list.

Batteries flatten and take electronics with them, which is why a conditioning charger is standard
advice rather than an accessory. Brake discs surface-corrode. Tyres flat-spot and age. Fluids
degrade on the calendar.

For a car doing 2,000 km a year, an annual inspection is worth considerably more than any
distance-triggered service.

## Hydraulic suspension

McLaren's interlinked hydraulic systems replace conventional anti-roll bars on many models. It
works superbly and it is genuinely specialist — diagnosis needs the right software, and the
components fail individually, so a workshop that can identify which one has failed saves you from
replacing an assembly.

## The tub

Structural work on a carbon monocoque is rare and highly specialised. Knowing which workshops can
even assess it properly is a large part of why the list of garages taking this make is as short
as it is.
