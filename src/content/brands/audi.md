Audi ownership in Dubai is straightforward if the right things are done on time, and expensive if
they are not. The list of right things is short and well documented.

## Approvals, not viscosities

VAG engines specify oil by approval — 504.00, 508.00 and others — and those codes describe
additive chemistry, particulate filter compatibility and shear stability rather than just
thickness.

Filling a car specifying 504.00 with a generic 5W-30 is one of the more common ways to cause
timing and emissions problems that then get chased electronically at considerable expense. The
approval should be printed on your invoice.

## The DSG interval

Wet-clutch DSG gearboxes need a fluid and filter change every 60,000 km. It is in the schedule,
it is not expensive, and it is missed constantly because owners are never told at the point of
sale.

A missed DSG service is the most common avoidable cause of a large gearbox bill on these cars in
this market.

## Known issues are actually known

Timing chains on certain engines, water pumps across several, and oil consumption on specific
2.0 TFSI variants are documented patterns rather than rumours.

The advantage of a VAG specialist is that they check whether yours is affected, and address it as
a planned job. A chain replaced on a schedule costs a fraction of one that jumped.

## Parts pricing

A great deal of an Audi is shared across the Volkswagen group, and the same component frequently
exists in a supplier's own box at a much lower price than the four-ring version. A workshop that
knows which parts those are will save you real money without compromising the repair.
