Nissan servicing in Dubai is inexpensive and widely available, with one item that decides whether
ownership stays that way.

## The CVT question

Most modern Nissan saloons and crossovers here use a continuously variable transmission, and CVTs
are more sensitive to fluid condition than conventional automatics. Heat degrades that fluid, and
this market supplies heat in quantity.

The interval that matters is a fluid service every 60,000 km, using genuine Nissan CVT fluid.
This is one of the few places on this site where a generic equivalent is a genuinely bad idea —
the wrong fluid produces shudder and accelerates belt and pulley wear.

Done on time, it is a few hundred dirhams. Skipped, it is the most common route to a gearbox
replacement on these cars.

## Patrols

The Y62 is a desert vehicle used as a family car, and both roles are hard on it in different
ways. Sand driving loads the cooling system at low airflow. Towing works the transmission.
Speed bumps and ramps work the suspension.

None of that is a fault; it just means inspection intervals matter more than the book figure.

## Where competition helps

Nissan is serviced well by most all-makes garages here, so routine work is genuinely competitive
and worth comparing. The exception is CVT work, which rewards a specialist and where the cheapest
quote is not automatically the one to take.
