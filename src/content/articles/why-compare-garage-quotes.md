We analysed quotes flowing through the Cars911 network and the spread is consistent: for identical jobs on identical cars, the highest quote is typically 40–60% above the lowest — sometimes from garages on the same street.

The difference isn't always quality. It's overheads, parts sourcing (genuine vs OEM vs aftermarket), and how busy the workshop is that week.

That's the whole reason Cars911 exists: describe the job once, let multiple verified garages answer, and choose on price, warranty, rating and turnaround — not on which garage happened to be nearest.
