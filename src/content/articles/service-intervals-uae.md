Manufacturer schedules are written for mild climates. In the UAE, heat, dust and stop-start traffic age oil, filters and rubber components faster than the handbook assumes.

## A realistic UAE schedule

- **Every 5,000–7,000 km or 6 months** — minor service: engine oil, oil filter, fluid top-ups, visual safety check.
- **Every 15,000 km or 12 months** — full service: add air filter, cabin filter, brake inspection, diagnostics scan.
- **Every 30,000–40,000 km** — major service: spark plugs, transmission fluid check, coolant, full inspection.

## Signs you should not wait for the next interval

- The AC is cooling noticeably less — a UAE summer will find the weak point fast.
- Any new vibration, pulling, or noise under braking.
- The oil on the dipstick is black and thin well before the next service is due.
