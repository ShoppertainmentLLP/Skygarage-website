Warning lights follow a traffic-light logic: amber means investigate soon, red means act now.

## Stop the car safely, then call for help

- **Oil pressure (red oil can)** — engine damage happens in minutes. Stop, switch off, call for assistance.
- **Temperature (red thermometer)** — overheating. Pull over before the head gasket pays the price.
- **Battery/charging (red battery)** — the car is running on stored charge only and will stall soon.

## Book a garage this week

- **Check engine (amber)** — hundreds of possible causes; a diagnostics scan reads the exact fault code.
- **ABS (amber)** — normal braking still works, but the anti-lock system is offline.
- **Tyre pressure** — in UAE heat, underinflated tyres fail fast. Check pressures the same day.
