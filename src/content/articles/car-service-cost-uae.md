Servicing prices in the UAE vary more than almost anywhere else, because dealer labour rates and independent workshop rates sit far apart for identical work.

## Typical ranges

- **Minor service** (oil, filter, fluid top-ups, safety check): AED 149–399
- **Interim service** for high-mileage drivers: AED 129–299
- **Full service** (all filters and fluids, brake check, diagnostics): AED 399–999
- **Major service** (manufacturer schedule including plugs): AED 499–1,499

The spread inside each band is mostly the car. A four-cylinder Corolla and a V8 Land Cruiser both need "a minor service", but the second takes more oil, a larger filter and longer on the ramp.

## Where the difference between quotes comes from

Three things, in order of size:

1. **Labour rate.** Dealer rates commonly run two to three times an independent workshop's, for the same hours of work.
2. **Parts grade.** Genuine, OEM and aftermarket parts can differ by half the total bill. A quote that does not say which you are getting is not a complete quote.
3. **What is actually included.** "Full service" is not a regulated term. One garage's version includes the cabin filter and a diagnostics scan; another's does not.

## How to compare fairly

Ask every garage the same three questions: which parts grade, what is included, and what the warranty covers. Once those are equal, the remaining difference is genuinely price — and on identical work the highest quote is typically 40–60% above the lowest.

That comparison is the whole point of sending one request to several garages rather than calling one and hoping.
