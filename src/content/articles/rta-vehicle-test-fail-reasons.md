The RTA vehicle test is a technical assessment to ensure a vehicle meets Dubai's roadworthiness and safety standards. A car may seem fine during daily use and still fail, because the inspection evaluates measurable conditions rather than the driver's familiarity with the car. Knowing about a defect does not make it less significant.

## Tyres

Tyres are critical for safety and legal compliance. Excessive tread wear, sidewall damage, cracks, bulges or mismatched specifications reduce grip and stability. Dubai's heat accelerates tyre deterioration and increases stress.

Owners often check only tread depth, but age, deformation and uneven wear may indicate suspension, alignment or inflation issues. Inspect all tyres, including the spare, as a system before testing.

## Brakes

A car that stops is not always braking correctly. Inspections may find uneven braking, worn pads or discs, fluid leaks, weak parking brakes or ABS warnings. Pulling to one side, vibration, a soft pedal or metallic noises all indicate safety issues. Diagnose these symptoms before testing, as replacing a single part may not fix the root cause.

## Lights and visibility

Failed bulbs are a common and avoidable cause of test failure. Make sure all lights work properly and are correctly aligned. Windscreen damage in the driver's view, ineffective wipers, poor washer performance, and illegal or damaged tinting can also cause failure.

Address dashboard warning lights by fixing the underlying issue, not just clearing the code — the warning will reappear if the fault remains.

## Steering, suspension and the underside

Gradual issues such as loose steering, worn bushes, leaking shock absorbers, damaged mountings and excessive play may go unnoticed by drivers but will be detected during inspection. Fluid leaks, exhaust damage, excessive emissions, abnormal noise or loose components can also cause failure. Mechanical integrity matters more than cosmetic appearance.

## Modifications and identification

Modifications such as non-standard exhausts, lighting or suspension changes may require approval, or be prohibited outright if they affect safety, emissions or compliance. Chassis and vehicle identification must remain legible and match registration records. Confirm that all modifications are permitted and properly documented before testing.

## Before you book

A thorough RTA pre-test check should cover tyres, brakes, steering, suspension, lights, visibility, fluid leaks, emissions, warning systems and vehicle identification. Since June 2025, appointments are generally required for inspections at Dubai testing centres, with limited exceptions and extra fees for walk-ins. Arriving with unresolved defects increases costs, wastes time and leads to repeat visits.

The most cost-effective approach is to identify defects early, get an accurate quote, and approve only necessary repairs.
