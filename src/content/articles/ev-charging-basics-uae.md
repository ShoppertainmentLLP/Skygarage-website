Electric cars need less routine maintenance than petrol cars — but not zero, and UAE conditions add their own considerations.

## Charging options

- **Home AC charging** — the cheapest way to run an EV; most owners charge overnight.
- **Public DC fast charging** — DEWA, ADDC and private networks cover the major routes; ideal for top-ups, not daily use.

## What servicing an EV involves

No oil changes, no spark plugs — but EVs still need tyre rotation (they wear faster under instant torque), brake fluid changes, cabin filters, coolant for the battery thermal system, and software/diagnostics checks. Battery health checks are worth doing annually in a hot climate.
