By July, AC repair wait times across Dubai double. Getting ahead of summer costs less and gets booked faster.

1. **Cooling performance test** — vent temperature should reach 6–10°C within minutes.
2. **Refrigerant level and leak check** — low gas is the most common cause of weak cooling.
3. **Compressor and belt inspection** — the hardest-working part of a UAE car.
4. **Cabin filter change** — dusty filters choke airflow long before the gas runs low.
5. **Condenser clean** — sand buildup makes the whole system fight itself.

Book an AC check now and it's a one-hour visit. Wait for the first 45°C week and it's a queue.
