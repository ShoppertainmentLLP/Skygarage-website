Dubai Investment Park is the workshop base for the whole southern corridor, and it serves a set
of communities that have almost no car trade of their own.

## Who uses it

Green Community, Expo City, Dubai South, the Gardens and Discovery Gardens all sit within easy
reach and contain very little automotive business. Between DIP and Jebel Ali, those areas are
covered.

## Fleet influence

A lot of light commercial and company vehicle work passes through here, and the effect on private
customers is generally positive. Workshops that serve fleets have to document what they did,
commit to turnaround and price transparently, because their contract customers audit all three.

That discipline does not switch off when a private car arrives.

## The commuting profile

Whichever direction people travel, they travel a long way. Residents head north into the city;
workers head south into DIP and Dubai South. Both patterns produce 30,000 km-plus years.

At that mileage the right cycle is a full service twice a year with an interim oil change
between, and closer attention to tyres, brake fluid and cooling than a low-mileage city car would
need.

## Practical advantages

It is genuinely easy to get to and park at, which the older industrial districts are not. If you
prefer to hand the car over yourself, look at the workshop and talk to whoever is doing the work,
this is one of the easier places in Dubai to do exactly that.
