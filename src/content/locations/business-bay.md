Business Bay is a district of people whose time costs more than the job does, and the servicing
market here has adapted to that completely.

## The pattern

Almost nobody drops a car off. Either a workshop collects it from the tower in the morning and
brings it back that afternoon, or a mobile mechanic does the work in the parking bay while the
car's owner is upstairs.

Both work because Al Quoz and Ras Al Khor — two of the city's largest workshop clusters — are a
few minutes away. Collection here genuinely means same-day on routine jobs.

## What Business Bay does to cars

Two things pull in opposite directions.

Basement parking is excellent for paint. Cars here keep their finish far better than anything
parked outside, which is why the detailing work in this area tends to be interior-focused.

Short journeys are hard on everything mechanical. A car that does a few kilometres a day never
fully recharges its battery, never gets its oil to temperature, and never really cleans its
brakes off. Batteries here fail early even by UAE standards.

## Valet damage

Worth naming because it is so common. Cars parked by other people accumulate kerbed wheels and
door dings at a much higher rate, and both are cheap to put right if you deal with several at
once — paintless dent removal is priced far better as a batch than as single visits.
