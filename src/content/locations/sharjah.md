Sharjah is where a lot of the region's difficult, labour-intensive car work quietly gets done,
and it is consistently the best value in the country for it.

## Why the pricing is different

Lower overheads, plainly. Rent, and to a degree labour, cost less than in Dubai, and that shows
up most on jobs where labour is the dominant cost: body and paint, engine work, gearbox rebuilds,
anything requiring many hours.

On a parts-dominated job the difference narrows, because a water pump costs what a water pump
costs wherever you fit it.

## What the industrial areas are good at

Depth on the makes that dominate the local car parc — Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Hyundai and Kia —
with parts widely stocked and technicians who see the same engines constantly.

There is also a substantial used and reconditioned parts trade here, which for an older car can
be the difference between a viable repair and writing it off. Worth asking about explicitly, and
worth asking what warranty comes with a used component.

## The traffic question

The honest constraint. The Dubai-Sharjah crossing at peak times can add an hour each way, twice,
which erodes a lot of the saving on a routine service.

For larger jobs it stops mattering, and most workshops here will collect from Dubai anyway. Ask
when you get the quote — collection is common and frequently free on a job of any size.

## Checking European coverage

The Japanese and Korean depth is genuine. For German marques, confirm the workshop holds proper
manufacturer-level diagnostic access rather than a generic reader before you book, because that
is where the variability is.
