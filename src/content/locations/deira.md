Deira is the oldest part of Dubai's motor trade and it works differently from the rest of the
city.

## Experience over equipment

The workshops here have been running for decades. They have worked on models that no longer exist
in the newer districts, and they remember the faults those cars develop.

For an older or discontinued vehicle, that institutional memory frequently beats a newer workshop
with better equipment and no familiarity. A mechanic who has rebuilt forty of your engine knows
where to look before anything is measured.

## The parts advantage

Deira has a genuine used and reconditioned parts trade. For a car whose new parts are either
unavailable or worth more than the vehicle, that is often the difference between a viable repair
and scrapping it.

The question worth asking is what warranty comes with a used part. Reputable suppliers here do
offer one, and the length of it tells you what they actually think of the component.

## The car parc shapes the work

More older, higher-mileage cars than anywhere else in the city, which means the work skews to
repair rather than maintenance: AC systems, cooling, suspension, electrical faults and exhausts
rather than scheduled services on cars under warranty.

## Parking outside

Far more cars sit in the sun here than in the tower districts, and it shows. Faded paint,
cracked dashboards, perished wiper blades and batteries that fail early are all the same cause.

None of it is expensive to manage — washing frequency, a sunshade, and an annual battery test
after the second year cover most of it — but ignoring it ages a car visibly fast.
