Jebel Ali's workshops grew up around JAFZA and the port, and they still carry that character:
organised, process-driven and priced for volume.

## Why fleet focus helps a private customer

A workshop whose main customers are companies running twenty vehicles has to be predictable. Jobs
are documented, turnaround is committed to, and pricing is transparent because a fleet manager
compares it monthly.

That discipline works in your favour when you bring one car in. It also means these workshops are
comfortable with scheduled maintenance rather than reactive repair, which is the right approach
for a high-mileage vehicle.

## The commuter profile

Cars from Dubai South, Discovery Gardens and the Gardens cover long motorway distances daily.
That changes the maintenance picture: service intervals arrive in months, tyres wear and age
under sustained high-speed heat, and brakes and cooling work harder than they would in the city.

An interim oil change between full services is genuinely worthwhile for these cars rather than a
sales line.

## Salt and industry

The combination of coastal air and industrial dust is harder on a car than either on its own.
Brake calliper slider pins seize, exhaust clamps corrode, and underbody fasteners rust in a way
that makes future repairs longer and more expensive.

The mitigation is cheap: insist that brake hardware is cleaned and greased at every service, and
have the underbody looked at annually rather than only when something starts making noise.

## Practicalities

Unlike Al Quoz at 5pm, this is an easy place to drive to and park at. If you prefer to hand the
car over in person and talk to the technician, that is realistic here in a way it is not in the
more congested industrial districts.
