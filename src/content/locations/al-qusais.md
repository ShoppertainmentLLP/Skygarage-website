Al Qusais is the workshop district for the north-east of Dubai, and it serves a very large
residential population that would otherwise be crossing the whole city.

## Who it serves

Mirdif, Muhaisnah, Al Nahda, Al Twar and the communities along the Sharjah border — plus a
substantial number of people who commute from Sharjah itself and would rather stop at the first
workshop cluster than continue to Al Quoz.

## What it does well

Routine mechanical work, and a lot of it. Servicing, brakes, suspension, AC and battery work on
Japanese and Korean cars, which is what most of the surrounding car parc consists of.

Technicians here see the same models constantly, and that familiarity is worth as much on a
Corolla or a Sunny as marque specialisation is on a BMW.

## Where to look elsewhere

For difficult or unusual work — a gearbox rebuild, a specialist marque, a colour-matched respray
on a complicated finish — Al Quoz has more depth. That is a genuine difference rather than a
reflection on the workshops here.

For a recent German car, the question to ask before booking is whether the workshop holds
manufacturer-level diagnostic access rather than a generic reader. Some here do; not all.

## Value

Labour rates sit below the central industrial districts, which shows up most on jobs where hours
dominate the bill. For routine servicing on an ordinary car, this is among the better value in
Dubai — and with collection available across the north-east, using it does not require the trip.
