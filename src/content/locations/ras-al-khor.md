Ras Al Khor is Dubai's second workshop district and, for a lot of jobs, its best value.

## What is here

Three industrial sub-areas holding mechanical workshops, body and paint shops, upholstery,
electrical specialists and a substantial used-parts trade. The density is second only to Al Quoz,
and the pricing is generally lower.

That price difference is mostly overheads rather than quality. As in every industrial district
here, the range runs from excellent to indifferent, which is why the audit and the warranty terms
matter more than the postcode.

## Body work in particular

This is where a lot of Dubai's accident and cosmetic repair actually happens. If you are getting
panel work or a respray quoted, it is worth having one quote from here alongside whatever else
you are considering — the gap on labour-heavy jobs can be substantial.

The questions that separate quotes are the same everywhere: is the colour matched to the car
rather than only to the code, is it blended into adjacent panels, and is it sprayed in a booth.

## Proximity

Ras Al Khor's real advantage over Al Quoz for a lot of people is location. Downtown, Business Bay
and Meydan are minutes away, which makes morning collection and afternoon return genuinely
achievable rather than aspirational.

## Visiting

It is a working industrial area with truck traffic and unit numbering that defeats most
first-time visitors. If you can have the car collected, do — most workshops here offer it, and it
removes the only real friction in using them.
