Al Quoz is where the rest of Dubai sends its cars. Four industrial sub-areas hold the densest
concentration of workshops in the UAE, and almost every specialist trade in the country has at
least one unit here.

That density is the point. Most parts of Dubai have all-makes garages that do everything
adequately. Al Quoz has workshops that do one thing constantly — one that only rebuilds German
automatic gearboxes, one that only refurbishes wheels, one that only does colour-matched paint —
and a specialist who sees the same job fifty times a year does it better and usually faster.

## The price spread is real

Two units on the same street can quote a two-fold difference on the same repair, and both can be
legitimate. One carries a spray booth, a diagnostic subscription and eight staff; the other is
three men and a hoist. For some jobs the difference matters enormously and for others it does not
matter at all.

This is why comparing quotes pays more in Al Quoz than anywhere else in the country, and why the
network shows you the warranty and the price band next to the price.

## What to bring it here for

Body and paint. Engine and gearbox work. Wheel and rim repair. Detailing, coating and paint
protection film. Anything where the answer is "you need someone who has done this before".

Routine servicing is available closer to home almost anywhere in Dubai, and often from a mobile
mechanic who comes to you. Al Quoz earns the trip when the job is difficult.

## Getting there, or not

The streets are industrial, the unit numbering is genuinely confusing and the traffic includes
trucks. Most workshops here run pick and drop across Dubai, and a large share of their customers
never visit at all. If your time is worth more than the trip, take the collection.
