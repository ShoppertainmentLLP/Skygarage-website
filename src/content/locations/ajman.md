Ajman's workshops are the value end of the UAE market, and for the right jobs the saving is real
rather than marginal.

## Where the saving comes from

Lower rent and lower labour rates, which means the difference shows up on jobs where hours
dominate the bill: body and paint work, engine and gearbox repairs, anything requiring many hours
of skilled time.

On a parts-heavy job the gap narrows considerably, because a component costs roughly the same
wherever it is fitted.

## The commuter factor

A large share of Ajman residents drive into Sharjah or Dubai daily. That is a lot of kilometres
in a year, and it means service intervals arrive on mileage well before they arrive on the
calendar.

For those cars a full service twice a year with an interim oil change between is the realistic
pattern, along with closer attention to tyres and brake fluid than a low-mileage car needs.

## Coastal corrosion

Ajman is coastal enough that salt matters. Brake calliper slider pins, exhaust clamps and
underbody fasteners corrode faster than inland, and the cheap countermeasure is the same as
everywhere on this coast: brake hardware cleaned and greased at every service rather than simply
measured.

## Choosing a workshop

The quality range here is as wide as the price range, which is why the network's audit —
licensing, insurance, facility, workmanship checks and written warranty terms — matters more in
the value end of the market than at the top of it.
