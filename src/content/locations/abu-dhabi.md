Abu Dhabi's car servicing market has the same shape as Dubai's, compressed into one industrial
area and stretched across longer distances.

## Mussafah

Almost the entire independent trade is here, in the numbered industrial sectors. Body shops,
mechanical specialists, tyre and wheel operations, marque specialists — the density is
comparable to Al Quoz, and the range of quality is just as wide.

It is also a genuine drive from the island, Reem or Saadiyat, and doing that drive twice for one
service is what pick and drop exists to solve. Most of the workshops here offer it as standard.

## The commuter factor

A significant number of cars in Abu Dhabi run the Dubai motorway daily. That is 250 to 300 km a
day, which reaches a service interval in a matter of months.

For those cars the useful pattern is a full service twice a year with an interim oil change
between, and closer attention than average to tyres, brakes and cooling — high-speed motorway
running in this heat is harder on all three than city mileage.

## Fleet influence

Abu Dhabi workshops handle a lot of fleet and government vehicles. It shows in the paperwork,
which is generally thorough, and occasionally in the lead times when a workshop is running a
contract.

Worth asking about turnaround when you book rather than assuming same-day.

## Getting comparable quotes

Same rule as anywhere: the labour rate, the parts specification and what is actually included.
Ask about all three and the remaining difference between two Mussafah quotes is genuinely price.
