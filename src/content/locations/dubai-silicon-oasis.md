Silicon Oasis cars do more kilometres than almost anywhere else in Dubai, and that single fact
should drive every maintenance decision made here.

## The mileage reality

A daily commute down the Dubai-Al Ain Road or across town easily reaches 30,000 to 40,000 km a
year. At that rate a car reaches a full-service interval in about six months and an oil change
well before that.

The pattern that works is a full service twice a year with an interim oil change between. It
sounds like more maintenance than the handbook asks for, and at this mileage it is simply the
handbook interval arriving sooner.

## Motorway wear is different

Worth understanding because it is counterintuitive.

High-speed running is gentler on brake pads and batteries than stop-start traffic — the brakes
are used less and the alternator has time to work.

It is considerably harder on tyres, cooling systems and brake fluid. Sustained speed in this heat
puts real energy into all three, which is why tyre condition and fluid intervals deserve more
attention on a commuter car than on a city one.

## The tyre point specifically

This is the driving profile where tyre failures actually happen in the UAE. An under-inflated or
ageing tyre at 120 km/h on a 45°C afternoon is the exact combination that produces a blowout.

Pressures monthly, checked cold. Sidewalls examined for fine cracking. Replaced on age at around
five years even with tread remaining.

## Local advantage

Unlike most Dubai residential areas, DSO has workshops inside it. For routine servicing on a car
that needs it twice a year, not having to cross the city is worth more than it sounds.
