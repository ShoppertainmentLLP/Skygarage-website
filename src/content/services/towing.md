Towing is bought under pressure, which is exactly why the terms should be settled before the
truck moves.

## Two things to fix on the phone

The price, and the destination.

The price, because a figure agreed on arrival — with your car immobile and the afternoon
disappearing — is not a negotiation. Distance, vehicle type and access determine it, and all
three are known before dispatch.

The destination, because a car delivered to a garage you did not choose puts you in the weakest
possible position on the repair that follows. Pick the workshop first. Every recovery in this
network goes wherever you name.

## Flatbed, in almost every case

A flatbed lifts the car completely clear of the road. A wheel-lift truck drags two wheels along
it, which is a real risk on all-wheel-drive cars and on many automatics, and a real risk to the
front bumper of anything low on a steep ramp.

The price difference is modest. The damage from getting it wrong is not.

## Say what the situation actually is

Whether the car rolls, whether it is in a basement, whether the steering locks, whether you are
on a hard shoulder. Each of those changes which truck and which equipment is sent, and a
dispatcher who knows in advance sends the right one the first time.

If you are on a motorway, get out of the car and behind the barrier while you wait. Nothing about
the car matters as much as that.

## Ask whether you need one at all

A flat battery, a puncture, or a fault a mobile mechanic can clear on the spot are all cheaper
and quicker than a recovery. Describe the symptom and a decent operator will tell you when a tow
is not the answer.
