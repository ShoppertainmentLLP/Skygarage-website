An inspection buys information, and information is the cheapest thing on this site relative to
what it saves.

## What an hour on a lift finds

Things you cannot see from the driveway. A weeping shock absorber. A coolant hose gone hard at
the clip. A tyre with 4 mm of tread and fine cracking across the sidewall because it is six years
old. Brake pads at 3 mm on one side and 6 mm on the other, which means a seized calliper slider
rather than worn pads. A sump gasket starting to seep.

None of those announce themselves. All of them are cheaper now than in three months.

## The scan half

Modern cars store faults that have not yet triggered a warning light — pending codes, sensors
drifting out of range, a module reporting low voltage overnight.

Reading them is the difference between finding a problem while it is a sensor and finding it when
it is a converter.

## Before the annual test

The UAE inspection fails cars for a fairly predictable list: tyres, brakes, lights, suspension
play, excessive emissions and undeclared modifications.

Every one of those is findable a fortnight beforehand. Doing that turns the test from an event
into a formality, and avoids the retest fee and the second trip.

## What you should get

A written report with measurements, not adjectives. "Front pads 4 mm, rear 7 mm, minimum 3 mm" is
a fact you can act on and compare quotes against. "Brakes worn" is a sales line.
