A full service sits between the routine oil change and the big scheduled overhaul, and for a lot
of cars in this country it is the sensible annual appointment.

## What it is for

The minor service keeps the oil fresh. The major service replaces the long-interval items. The
full service is the annual proper look at everything that wears: all the filters, the condition
of every fluid, the actual thickness of the brakes, the age of the tyres, and what the car's
computers have been quietly recording.

In a climate that ages filters and fluids faster than the schedules assume, that yearly look is
worth more here than the mileage figure suggests.

## Measurements are the point

The most useful thing you can leave with is numbers.

Front pads 5 mm. Rear pads 7 mm. Discs 24.2 mm against a 22 mm minimum. Brake fluid at 2%
moisture. Front tyres manufactured 2023, 5 mm tread.

Next year those figures tell you the wear rate, which tells you what to budget and when. Compare
that with "brakes getting low", which is what you get told every year regardless.

## Comparing quotes

Because the scope is defined, the prices mean something. What still varies is the oil
specification, the number of litres and whether the cabin filter is included — three questions
worth asking when two quotes are a few hundred dirhams apart.

## The approval rule

Anything found during the inspection should be quoted and approved before it happens. A service
that turns into a much larger invoice without a phone call is the most common complaint in this
industry anywhere, and it is the thing this network is set up to prevent.
