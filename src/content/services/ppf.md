Paint protection film is the only product on this site that physically stops damage. Everything
else in the protection category is chemical.

## Why it makes particular sense here

Dubai has a lot of construction traffic and a lot of sand on the road, and both throw material at
the front of your car constantly. The bumper, the leading edge of the bonnet, the mirror faces
and the headlights take almost all of it.

That distribution is the useful fact when deciding coverage. A front-end pack costs a fraction of
a full-car job and covers the large majority of the risk. Full-car film is a genuine choice for a
car you intend to keep immaculate, but it should be a choice rather than the default quote.

## What separates installers

Three things, all invisible in a price list.

Preparation, because film locks in whatever is under it. Edge treatment, because film wrapped
around a panel edge has no visible line and will not lift, while film trimmed on the flat leaves
a seam that collects dirt. And environment, because dust settling under film during installation
is permanent.

## Film quality is not marketing

Quality TPU film has a self-healing topcoat — light marks disappear with heat — and UV inhibitors
that keep it clear. Cheap PVC-based film in this sun yellows, and on a white car the boundary
where the film ends becomes obvious within two summers.

The warranty term is the shorthand: reputable film carries five to ten years against yellowing
and delamination.

## Film and coating together

They are complementary rather than alternatives, and most studios here will suggest film on the
front and a coating over everything, including over the film. That combination is what people are
actually buying when they say they want their car protected.
