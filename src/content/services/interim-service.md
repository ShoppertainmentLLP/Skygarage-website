An interim service is the answer to a specific problem: manufacturer intervals assume a car and a
climate that are not yours.

## Who it is for

Anyone commuting between emirates, anyone doing 25,000 km a year or more, and anyone whose car
lives in stop-start traffic and short trips.

For a car doing 8,000 km a year, an annual full service is sufficient and an interim service is
money you do not need to spend. Any workshop worth using will tell you that rather than sell you
one.

## Why oil ages faster here

Three reasons, and they compound.

High ambient temperature oxidises oil. Idling in traffic accumulates engine hours that the
odometer never records — an hour crawling on Sheikh Zayed Road is engine wear with almost no
distance attached. And short trips never bring the oil fully up to temperature, so fuel dilution
and moisture stay in the sump instead of evaporating off.

A car that looks lightly used by mileage can have oil in considerably worse condition than the
number suggests.

## Keeping it simple

The interim service is deliberately narrow: oil, filter, fluids, a safety check. It is quick, it
is cheap, and it is easy to keep up with — particularly if a mobile mechanic does it at your
building, which removes the only real reason people skip it.

## Recording it

Have it entered in the service record. A history showing more frequent oil changes than the
schedule required reads well to the next owner in a market where buyers are rightly suspicious of
heat-damaged engines.
