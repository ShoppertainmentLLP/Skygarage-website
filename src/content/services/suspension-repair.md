Suspension is the system people tolerate for longest before fixing, usually because it degrades
slowly enough that you adapt to it without noticing.

## What Dubai does to it

Speed bumps at every junction, steep basement ramps, and after any rain a crop of potholes on
roads that were fine the week before. Add heat, which hardens rubber bushes faster, and the
result is that bushes, drop links and top mounts here reach the end of their lives well before
the mileage suggests.

Air-sprung cars have their own version. Compressors run more often in heat, air springs perish
where rubber meets heat and UV, and one component failing quietly loads the others.

## It is not a comfort item

A worn damper does not just make the ride floaty. It lets the tyre lose contact with the road
over undulations, which lengthens braking distance and scallops the tread into an uneven pattern
that then makes noise you will blame on the tyres.

That is the argument for fixing it: it costs you tyres and stopping distance, not just comfort.

## Diagnosis on air suspension

A car sitting low on one corner overnight is the classic symptom, and it has four common causes
at wildly different prices: a leaking air spring, a failing compressor, a stuck valve block, or a
bad height sensor.

Any quote produced without testing which one it is will be priced at the expensive end, because
that is the only safe way to guess.

## Finish the job

Every suspension repair changes the geometry, and geometry that is out will scrub a new set of
tyres in a fraction of their life. Alignment afterwards is not an upsell — check that it is in
the quote.
