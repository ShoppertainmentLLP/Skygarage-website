Small dents are the most common cosmetic damage in Dubai and the one with the widest gap between
a cheap repair and a good one.

## Two completely different repairs

Paintless dent removal works the metal back to shape from behind, or pulls it from the front with
glue tabs. Nothing is filled and nothing is sprayed, so the panel keeps its factory paint. When
the conditions allow it, it is both cheaper and better.

Conventional repair fills, primes, sprays and blends. It is the right answer when paint is broken
or metal is creased, and a good body shop produces a result you cannot find. But it is a respray,
and original paint never comes back.

The conditions that decide between them are objective: is the paint intact, is the metal
stretched, and is the dent on or near a body line. Any shop should be able to answer all three
while you watch.

## Why the estimates differ so much

Because the two methods cost very different amounts, and quotes rarely say which one is being
proposed.

If one quote is AED 250 and another is AED 1,200, the useful question is not who is cheaper — it
is whether the expensive one is planning to spray a panel that could have been pushed out.

## The car-park reality

Basement bays here are narrow, trolleys are everywhere, and door strikes are simply part of
owning a car in a tower. Most cars accumulate several small dings a year.

They are much cheaper dealt with together, and PDR technicians price a multi-dent job well below
the sum of individual ones.

## Insurance maths

For a AED 600 dent with a AED 1,000 excess, claiming makes no sense. For anything involving
another vehicle or structural damage, it does. A body shop that says so before you file is
telling you against its own short-term interest, which is worth remembering.
