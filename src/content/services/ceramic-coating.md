Ceramic coating is one of the few products in car care where the marketing and the reality
diverge sharply, and where the difference between two quotes at the same price is enormous.

## What you are actually buying

A coating is a thin, hard, semi-permanent layer chemically bonded to the clear coat. It resists
chemical etching, sheds water, and makes the surface far easier to clean.

It does not stop stone chips. It does not stop a trolley. It does not make a car scratch-proof.

In Dubai the honest case for it is specific: hard water spotting, bird lime that etches within
hours in summer sun, and constant dust that has to be washed off week after week. A coating makes
each of those less damaging and each wash gentler and quicker.

## The prep is the job

The bottle is a small part of the cost. The hours are in correction.

Because a coating seals the surface as it finds it, every swirl mark and every water spot present
on the day is locked in underneath for years. That is why a proper job is correct-then-coat, and
why a studio offering to coat a car same-day for a few hundred dirhams is selling you a
preserved version of what you already have.

## Curing is not optional

Coatings need time and controlled conditions to cure. In this climate that means indoors, out of
direct heat, usually overnight and often longer.

A car coated on a forecourt in Dubai afternoon heat has a coating that flashed off before it
levelled. It will look acceptable for a month.

## The warranty question

Most multi-year warranties require pH-neutral washing and periodic inspection at the studio.
Those conditions are reasonable, but read them: a five-year claim that lapses if you use an
automatic wash once is worth knowing about before you pay for five years.
