Detailing in Dubai is less a luxury than a repair, because the things that damage paint here work
constantly and are all cumulative.

Sun degrades the clear coat. Fine sand acts as an abrasive every time the car is wiped. Hard
water dries into mineral deposits that etch. And the most common single cause of dull paint on a
Dubai car is not the environment at all — it is being washed with brushes or dirty cloths, week
after week, until the surface is covered in fine circular scratches that scatter light.

## What correction actually is

Machine polishing removes a measured amount of clear coat so that the surface becomes flat again,
which is why light reflects cleanly off it afterwards.

That is worth understanding because clear coat is finite. A car can only be properly corrected a
handful of times in its life. A studio that measures paint depth, tells you which defects it will
not chase, and leaves something in reserve is doing you a favour, even though it sounds like
doing less.

The alternative — a filler glaze that hides swirls for a month and washes out — is what a cheap
"polish" often means.

## The interior half

Underrated, and in this climate the part that ages a car fastest.

Sand works into seat rails, seat-belt reels, air vents and the boot lip and stays there. Leather
dries and cracks under sustained heat if it is never conditioned. Plastics fade. Air-con systems
push dust through a cabin filter into the vents and, eventually, into the smell of the car.

An interior detail with proper extraction is the difference between a five-year-old car that
feels old and one that does not.

## Sequencing it properly

If you are considering ceramic coating or paint protection film, the detail comes first and is
not optional. Both lock the surface in as it is — every swirl, every water spot — for years.

Correction, then protection, in that order. Any studio that offers to coat a car without
correcting it first is telling you something about how it works.
