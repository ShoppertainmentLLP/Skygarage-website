A mobile mechanic is not a compromised version of a garage. For a specific and fairly large set
of jobs, it is simply the better way to have them done.

The set is defined by physics, not ambition. Anything that can be done with the car on the ground
or on a single jack stand, with tools that fit in a van, is a mobile job: servicing, filters,
batteries, alternators, starters, brake pads and discs, suspension links, diagnostics, AC
regassing. Anything needing the car level in the air on four points, or an engine or gearbox out,
is not.

## Why it suits Dubai specifically

Most of the cost of a garage visit here is not the invoice. It is the two hours of driving,
waiting and returning, usually taken out of a working day, often twice if the parts are not in
stock.

The car also spends most of its life in a basement bay, which is a shaded, level, well-lit place
to work — better in July than a lot of open workshop yards.

## The warning-light call

The most common reason people book a mobile mechanic is a light on the dash and no idea whether
it is serious.

A scan takes fifteen minutes and answers the only question that matters: drive it, book it, or
stop driving it. Most teams credit the diagnostic fee against the repair if you go ahead, which
means the honest answer costs you nothing when it is bad news and very little when it is not.

## What to check before booking

Ask what happens to the old oil — it should leave with the mechanic for a licensed handler. Ask
whether the parts are on the van or being ordered, because that decides whether it is one visit
or two. And ask what they will do if the fault turns out to need a workshop, since the good teams
will hand you a diagnosis and a referral rather than an invoice for a failed attempt.
